# Code of Conduct

{{name}} follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
we want a community free of harassment, where people of every background are welcome, disagree respectfully and
look after each other. It applies in all of {{name}}'s spaces – the repositories, issues, discussions and events –
and where someone represents the project.

Report behaviour that breaks it to the maintainers at [support@falkoss.org](mailto:support@falkoss.org) or through a
private report in the repository's *Security* tab. Reports are handled confidentially; the maintainers decide on
consequences as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
