# Support

- **Questions and ideas:** open an issue in the repository it is about.
- **Bugs:** an issue with what you did, what happened and what you expected, and the version.
- **Security problems:** never in public – see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Everything else:** [support@falkoss.org](mailto:support@falkoss.org).

{{name}} is free software made by volunteers; there is no paid support tier – everything is included.
